What it does

Six things, built properly.

Balanced postings, append-only

Every transaction debits and credits real accounts. Mistakes are corrected by a reversal, never by an edit, so the history stays auditable. The books check on every load that assets − liabilities = equity + income − expenses.

Statement ingestion that reconciles

PDF, JSON and CSV bank and card statements. Four duplicate checks — same file, same row, same bank reference, looks-the-same-within-a-week — and every row stages for review against the bank's own totals before it reaches the ledger.

Indian capital-gains lots

FIFO lot matching with holding-period buckets, STCG and LTCG tiering, the ₹1,25,000 LTCG exemption per financial year, and realised-gains statements scoped to the FY.

XIRR on real cashflows

Money-weighted return from your dated contributions and withdrawals. When the inputs cannot support a figure you get an em-dash and the reason, never a zero dressed up as an answer.

The full Indian charge stack

Brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover fee, stamp duty, GST and DP charges stored per trade and carried into cost basis and realised gain.

Loans, cards, deposits, gold

EMI schedules with the principal and interest split taken from the amortisation, card cycles and minimum due, FD, RD, PPF, EPF and NPS accrual, and digital gold with lease credits tracked in grams.

Four steps from a bank statement to a tax figure.

NSE and BSE equities today; AMFI scheme NAVs are in progress, so fund holdings are valued from the price you last entered. Every figure carries its price state and date.

Net worth, realised gains by financial year, budget variance, payoff comparisons. Export the numbers you need for your return.

What it is not

No streaming ticker, no signals, no advice.

—There is no real-time quote feed. Prices are fetched on request and stamped Priced, Stale, Unpriced or FX unavailable.
—Mutual-fund NAVs have no automatic fetch path yet. AMFI scheme codes are stored and the adapter is in progress; until it lands, fund values come from the price you last entered.
—There is no AI signal engine and no buy or sell recommendation. Categorisation runs on keyword rules you write and can read.
—Tax figures are indicative, computed against a named rule set with an effective date. Verify against the Finance Act before you file.
—Corporate actions are logged by you, not inferred. Any split-adjusted series says so on the chart.
